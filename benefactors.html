Benefactors

With heartfelt thanks to those who stand with our military families

Thank You

Your generosity changes lives

To every benefactor of the Landings Military Family Relief Fund — thank you. Your generosity is the reason a military family can keep the lights on during an emergency, put gas in the tank to reach a medical appointment, or put food on the table when times are hardest. Behind every gift is a real family facing a real moment of need, and your support meets them there with dignity and speed.

Because of benefactors like you, help arrives within 24 to 48 hours, given as a grant that never has to be repaid. Your contribution doesn't just ease a financial burden — it tells the families of those who serve that their community has not forgotten them. That is a powerful and lasting gift, and we are deeply grateful for it.

Our Benefactors

Our benefactors are our heroes

Every gift this Fund has ever received came from someone who decided a military family's hard week was their business too.

We are grateful for every donation we have received, going back to our first. Some were large. Many were not. The Fund has never measured a benefactor by the size of the check. A gift of fifty dollars buys a tank of gas for a soldier driving to a sick parent. A gift of five thousand keeps a young family in their home. Both arrive at the same door, and both matter.

We call our benefactors heroes, and we mean it plainly. The men and women we serve wear the uniform. Our benefactors are the ones standing behind them, quietly, year after year. That is its own kind of service.

We Intend to Recognize Those Who Give

This page is where we will do it. We are building a record of the individuals, families, and organizations who have supported this Fund, and we will publish those names here. Anyone who prefers to give anonymously will always be honored in that wish. Recognition is offered, never required.

Please Consider a Gift

The need does not pause. Soldiers and their families at Hunter Army Airfield and Fort Stewart come to us with emergencies that will not wait for a fundraising cycle. We answer most requests within 24 to 48 hours. We can only do that because money is already in hand when the call comes.

Assistance is given as a grant, not a loan. No family is ever asked to repay help received in a crisis. Every request is reviewed, documented, and verified through the service member's command before a dollar moves. Your money goes to the family, and we can show you how.

If you have given before, thank you. We would be grateful if you would give again. If you have not yet, this is a good place to start.
