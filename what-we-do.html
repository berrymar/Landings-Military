Our Programs

Types of Assistance

LMFRF support is built around real, immediate needs — especially those that fall outside existing military resources.

⚠

Emergency Financial Crisis Response

Rapid grants to help military families through sudden, unexpected financial hardships when time is critical.

⛽

LMFRF Gas Cards

Fuel assistance so families can get to work, medical appointments, and essential errands without added stress.

🍲

Soldier Food Voucher Program

Food vouchers that ensure service members and their families have access to the meals they need.

🎒

Fort Stewart Backpack Buddies

Support for the children of military families, helping them stay nourished and ready to learn.

⚀

Therapeutic Devices

Assistance obtaining essential medical and therapeutic equipment that improves quality of life.

👶

Compassionate Care Transportation

Travel assistance for the birth of a child, easing the way during one of life's most important moments.

Need Soldier Food Vouchers or Gas Cards?

If you or a service member you know needs Soldier Food Vouchers or LMFRF Gas Cards, help is available. Reach out to begin the process — our team is here to support you.
