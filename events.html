Featured Event

Annual Landings Military Family Relief Fund Golf Classic

A day on the course in support of those who serve.

Join us for the Annual Landings Military Family Relief Fund Golf Classic, our signature fundraising event. Every dollar raised goes directly to providing rapid financial assistance to military families during emergencies — the gas cards, food vouchers, and emergency grants that make a real difference.

Date

15 March 2027

Location

Palmetto Clubhouse, The Landings

Format

Four-person scramble

The day typically includes a shotgun start, on-course contests, lunch, and an awards reception — plus plenty of opportunities to support the Fund through sponsorships and raffles.
