About the Fund

Who we are and why we exist

Our Mission

Rapid financial assistance when it matters most

The Landings Military Family Relief Fund (LMFRF) provides rapid financial assistance to military families to alleviate burdens during emergencies. Our grants focus on the needs that often fall outside of existing military resources — the gaps where families can find themselves without support.

Since its founding in 2007, the Fund has assisted more than 9,122 people. Support is given by way of a grant and does not require repayment, ensuring that families can focus on recovery rather than debt.

Our Values

  • Speed. Emergencies don't wait, and neither do we. Most applications are approved within 24–48 hours.
  • Dignity. We treat every service member and family with respect throughout the process.
  • Focus. We concentrate on the urgent, practical needs that other programs may not cover.
  • Community. We are powered by the generosity of donors who believe in supporting those who serve.

Who We Serve

LMFRF supports military service members and their families in the Coastal Empire region of Georgia, including those connected to Fort Stewart and surrounding communities. Assistance is available to service members facing emergencies who complete an application and intake process.

Our Benefit Administrator

As our benefit administrator, the United Way Coastal Empire brings trusted, experienced stewardship to the process. Their professional caseworkers verify eligibility, evaluate each request fairly and confidentially, and ensure that funds are directed quickly and responsibly to the families who need them most. This partnership allows the Fund to deliver help with both speed and accountability — most approvals are completed within 24–48 hours — while giving donors confidence that every gift is administered with care and integrity.

Learn more about our benefit administrator at United Way of the Coastal Empire.

Philanthropic Organization

The Savannah Community Foundation serves as the philanthropic home of the Landings Military Family Relief Fund. As a trusted community foundation, it holds and stewards the Fund's charitable assets, provides 501(c)(3) tax-deductible giving for donors, and processes contributions securely. Its professional staff manages the financial side of the Fund — receipting gifts, safeguarding the assets, and ensuring sound, transparent stewardship — so that the Fund can stay focused on its mission of delivering rapid relief to military families. This partnership gives donors confidence that their generosity is handled with integrity and lasting impact.

Learn more about our philanthropic partner at The Savannah Community Foundation.
