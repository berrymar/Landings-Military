Our History

Nearly two decades of standing with military families

Since 2007

A community commitment to those who serve

The Landings Military Family Relief Fund was established in 2007 with a clear purpose: to provide rapid financial assistance to military families during emergencies. From the beginning, the Fund focused on the urgent needs that often fall outside of existing military resources — the gaps where a family might otherwise have nowhere to turn.

What began as a local effort to support service members and their families has grown into a trusted source of relief. Over the years, the Fund has expanded its programs to meet evolving needs, from emergency crisis grants to gas cards, food vouchers, and support for children of military families.

Our Impact

Since 2007, the Fund has assisted more than 12,520 people. Each of those numbers represents a service member or family member who received help during a difficult time — help given as a grant, with no expectation of repayment.

Milestones

  • 2007 — The Landings Military Family Relief Fund is established to provide emergency assistance to military families.
  • Program growth — Added gas cards, the Soldier Food Voucher Program, and therapeutic device assistance.
  • Fort Stewart Backpack Buddies — Expanded support to children of military families.
  • Today — The Fund continues to deliver rapid relief, typically approving applications within 24–48 hours.
