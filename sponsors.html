Our Sponsors

Thank you to the partners who make our mission possible

With Gratitude

Generosity that reaches every military family we serve

The Landings Military Family Relief Fund is powered by the commitment of our sponsors. Their support funds the emergency grants, gas cards, food vouchers, and programs that bring rapid relief to military families in their time of need.

We Are Seeking Sponsors

Every grant we make begins with a sponsor. When a service member comes to us, the need is rarely abstract. It is rent that is due now. It is a car that will not start. It is a flight home for a funeral. We are able to answer most requests within 24 to 48 hours because sponsors put the money in place before the emergency arrives.

Few organizations sit where this Fund sits. We are rooted in The Landings, a community of residents who give generously and stay involved. We work directly alongside Hunter Army Airfield and Fort Stewart, home to the 3rd Infantry Division and thousands of soldiers and their families. That footprint lets a sponsor reach two audiences at once: an engaged residential community in Savannah and the military installations that anchor the Coastal Empire.

Where Your Support Goes

  • Title Sponsors. Title sponsorship underwrites the Fund's work for the full year. It carries the broadest recognition we offer, across our events, our communications, and this website. It is the most direct way for an organization to stand behind military families in this region.
  • Annual Golf Classic Sponsors. The Golf Classic is our signature event and a cornerstone of our annual fundraising. Sponsorships are available at several levels, from presenting sponsor to hole and hospitality sponsorships. It is a full day in front of Landings residents, local business leaders, and military guests.
  • Scholarship Sponsors. Our scholarships help military spouses finish their education at Georgia Southern University and Savannah Technical College. A degree earned is a household made more stable for good. Scholarship sponsors fund tuition directly, one semester at a time.
  • Program Sponsors. Program sponsorships support the work that runs year round. That includes gas cards, food vouchers, Backpack Buddies weekend meals for children at Fort Stewart, and equine therapy for service members managing PTSD and anxiety.

Your Money, Well Used

We keep this simple on purpose. The Fund operates with low overhead and a volunteer board. Assistance is given as a grant, not a loan, so no family is asked to repay help received in a crisis. Every request is reviewed, documented, and verified through the service member's command before a dollar moves.

We would welcome the chance to talk with you about which level fits your organization. Sponsorship here is not a donation that disappears into a general fund. It is a commitment that shows up at a kitchen table in Hinesville or Savannah, usually within two days.
