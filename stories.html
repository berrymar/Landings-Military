The families behind the mission

Real Impact

Lives changed by your generosity

Behind every grant is a family facing a difficult moment. These stories show what your support makes possible. More are being added — check back soon.

❝

A Family's Story

My husband deployed in the spring, and by June our youngest was in and out of the hospital and the bills had gotten ahead of me. The Fund covered our rent and our power that month. No loan, no repayment, no one making me feel small for asking. I got to sit with my son instead of worrying about the mailbox.

— An appreciative wife

❝

A Soldier's Story

I got the call at two in the morning. My mother had been hurt badly in a wreck back home and I had no leave saved and nothing in the account until payday. My first sergeant walked me through the paperwork, and two days later the Fund had me on a plane. I made it to her in time. I will not forget that.

— SP4, 3rd ID

❝

A Scholarship Story

Six moves in nine years meant I kept starting a degree and leaving it behind. The scholarship let me enroll at Savannah Tech and actually finish. I walked across that stage with my husband and both of our kids in the audience. It is the first thing in a long time that belonged to me.

— An appreciative spouse
