Our Mission

Rapid relief for those who serve

Established in 2007, the Landings Military Family Relief Fund (LMFRF) alleviates financial burdens for military families during emergencies. Support is given as a grant and never has to be repaid.

★

Rapid Response

Applications are typically approved within 24–48 hours so help arrives when families need it most.

❤

Grants, Not Loans

Assistance is provided as a grant. Families receive support with no expectation of repayment.

⚔

Fills the Gaps

We focus on urgent needs that often fall outside of existing military assistance programs.

How We Help

Types of Assistance

From emergencies to everyday essentials, LMFRF support takes many forms.

  • Emergency Financial Crisis Response Immediate help during unexpected hardships.
  • LMFRF Gas Cards Keeping families moving when transportation is a barrier.
  • Soldier Food Voucher Program Ensuring no service member or family goes hungry.
  • Fort Stewart Backpack Buddies Program Supporting children of military families.
  • Therapeutic Devices Assistance with essential medical and therapeutic equipment.
  • Compassionate Care Transportation Travel assistance for the birth of a child.
  • Military Spouse Scholarship Program Higher Education Tuition and Fees
