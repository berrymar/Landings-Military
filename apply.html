Get Help

How to apply for emergency assistance

How to Apply

Applying is straightforward, and help comes quickly

Service members requesting emergency assistance complete an application and a brief intake process. Most approvals are completed within 24–48 hours. Support is given by way of a grant and does not require repayment.

The Application Process

  1. Complete the application provided by The United Way

    Fill out the assistance application describing your situation and the help you need.

  2. Obtain required signatures

    Your application must include signatures from your supervisor and command.

  3. Complete an intake interview

    Meet with a caseworker for a short intake interview to review your needs.

  4. Provide supporting documentation

    Be ready to provide documentation such as your most recent EOM LES and a copy of your military ID.

  5. Receive a decision — usually within 24–48 hours

    Once reviewed, approved support is provided as a grant with no repayment required.

What You'll Need

  • A completed application
  • Signatures from your supervisor and command
  • Your most recent EOM LES (Leave and Earnings Statement)
  • A copy of your military ID

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can get help from the Landings Military Family Relief Fund?

Active-duty service members and their families stationed at Fort Stewart and Hunter Army Airfield who are facing a financial emergency.

What kinds of emergencies can the Fund help with?

Sudden, unexpected hardships that fall outside other military assistance programs. Past help has covered rent, utilities, emergency travel to be with family, and travel for the birth of a child. The Fund also provides gas cards and Soldier Food Vouchers.

Do I have to pay the money back?

No. Assistance is given as a grant, and it never has to be repaid.

How fast can I get help?

Most applications are approved within 24 to 48 hours of a complete application.

How do I apply?

Complete the application provided by the United Way of the Coastal Empire, which reviews every request for the Fund. You'll get the signatures you need, complete a short intake interview with a caseworker, and provide your documents. Call 211 or email landingsmilitaryrelief@uwce.org to get started.

What documents will I need?
  • A completed application, provided by The United Way
  • Signatures from your supervisor and command
  • Your most recent end-of-month LES (Leave and Earnings Statement)
  • A copy of your military ID
Is my request kept confidential?

Yes. United Way caseworkers review each request fairly and confidentially. The process does require the service member to go through their Chain of Command to receive assistance.

I already went to Army Emergency Relief (AER). Can the Fund still help?

Yes. The Fund focuses on urgent needs that existing military programs don't cover, so families who don't qualify for other aid, or who need more than it provides, are encouraged to apply.

How much assistance can I receive?

Each request is reviewed individually based on the need.

How do I get gas cards or Soldier Food Vouchers?

Call 211 or email the United Way team at landingsmilitaryrelief@uwce.org, and they'll walk you through the process.

Need emergency help, Soldier Food Vouchers, or LMFRF Gas Cards? Reach out to our team to get started, or call 211 for assistance and referrals.
