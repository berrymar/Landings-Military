Scholarship Program

Helping military spouses pursue their education in the Coastal Empire

Investing in Education

Supporting the dreams of military spouses

Through its scholarship program, the Landings Military Family Relief Fund helps spouses of active duty service members continue their education at local institutions. These scholarships ease the financial burden of tuition so that military families can build toward a brighter future, even amid the demands of military life.

The Fund partners with two respected schools in the region: Georgia Southern University and Savannah Technical College.

Georgia Southern University

With support from the Landings Military Family Relief Fund, scholarships are available to a select number of spouses of active duty service members.

To be eligible, applicants generally must have completed one term as a full-time student and be in good academic standing with the University. Recipients may attend any Georgia Southern campus — Savannah, Hinesville, or Statesboro.

Point of contact for applications: Rudy Duero, rduero@georgiasouthern.edu

Savannah Technical College

The Landings Military Family Relief Fund / MyCAA Scholarship supports active duty spouses using MyCAA funds, providing grants once MyCAA funds are no longer available to cover tuition in the student's current program of study.

Grants are provided one semester at a time. Students should request the grant in the term prior to running out of MyCAA funds.

Point of contact for applications: Jarred Keffer, jkeffer@savannahtech.edu

A track record of success

The program has helped dozens of military spouses stay enrolled and reach graduation. Of the scholarship recipients to date, the overwhelming majority are either still progressing in their programs or have already graduated — a testament to what this support makes possible.
